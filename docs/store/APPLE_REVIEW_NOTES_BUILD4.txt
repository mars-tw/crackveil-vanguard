Build 4 (version 1.0.0) replaces build 3 and adds eleven rounds of gameplay and readability work since our last reply.

HOW TO REVIEW: Main menu > 三陣封隙・出擊 (Start). Move with the bottom-left joystick. A rift appears on the ground with its required formation shown on it (需：楔形 / 圓陣 / 散兵). Tap the formation button (bottom right, above 旋斬) to switch, let the attack wave pass, then bring the captain and two squadmates into the rift for six seconds to seal it. Seal three rifts to summon the gate guardian. No account, purchase or network connection is needed.

ANSWERS TO YOUR QUESTIONS

1. What the app does: a single-player, offline action-strategy game in which you lead a squad of 3 to 9 heroes. Each stage opens three rifts in turn, each guarded by one of three attack patterns: a shield wall pushing from the front, a two-sided pincer, or artillery that marks landing points. Each pattern has one counter formation (Wedge, Ring or Scatter), marked on the ground. With the right formation the captain and two squadmates must hold the rift for six seconds between waves to seal it; with the wrong one, standing in the rift backfires and resets the seal. Sealing all three summons the gate guardian. A run takes two to three minutes. Recruited heroes form bonds tied to formations, and before each run the player picks a starting lineup and a starting node that trades a benefit for a cost.

2. Intended users: players aged 12+ who like short real-time sessions with a clear tactical decision each wave, without accounts, ads, purchases or network access.

3. Gap addressed: in most auto-attack survival games you control one character and only choose upgrades. Here each wave asks you to read the attack pattern, switch the whole squad into the counter formation and physically bring squadmates into the rift. Stages cannot be cleared by damage upgrades alone.

4. Beta testing: we have not yet received external tester feedback. A public TestFlight group exists but currently has no testers, and an Android closed test is set up without confirmed active testers. This build's changes come from our own documented playtest and review rounds (eleven since the last submission), for example: on-screen labels no longer overlap, so enemy names, rule text and seal rewards stay readable; a correct-formation result is no longer shown in the same red as a wrong one; dense line effects that hid artillery landing points were removed.

5. Standalone or suite: a standalone product. Our account's other games are different genres on different codebases; none is a squad-formation action game.

6. Inside another app: no. No other app on our account shares its genre, engine project, controls or content.

7. Shared code or assets with our other apps: none. It is its own Godot 4.7 project. Third-party components only: Godot Engine (MIT), Noto Sans CJK (SIL OFL) and a few CC0 assets from Kenney and OpenGameArt listed in the in-app credits. Parts of the code, the original 2D art and the music were made with AI-assisted tools under the developer's direction; the developer designed, reviewed, edited and integrated everything, and provenance is recorded in the public source repository.

8. Shared codebase or SDK with third parties: no SDK, template, analytics or advertising library beyond the components in answer 7.

9. Built for a client: no. The account holder conceived, built and publishes it as their own original product.

Known limitation: the game is landscape-first; on iPad held in portrait it shows black bars above and below the game area.

Thank you for your time.
