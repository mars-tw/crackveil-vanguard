## 繁體中文（台灣）

- **名稱**：裂隙先鋒：三陣封隙
- **副標題（30 字內）**：帶隊換陣，讓隊友站上裂隙才能封住
- **宣傳文字**：敵人用盾牆正推、兩翼包抄、遠射點名。看清楚這波是哪一種，換對陣形，帶兩名隊友站進裂隙。

**描述**

你帶的不是一個人，是一整隊。

每位隊員有自己的武器、生命和站位。按 Q（手機點陣形鈕）在楔形、圓陣、散兵之間切換，隊員會實際跑到新的位置。

裂隙會分三道打開。每道都會配一種攻勢：
- 盾牆正面推進：用楔形突破
- 兩翼包抄：用圓陣把隊友收在隊長身邊，受擊從 14 降到 4
- 遠射鎖定落點：用散兵分開站

危險區上會直接標出該用哪個陣形。陣形不對時，隊長站上裂隙會被反噬，進度歸零；換對了，等攻勢過去，帶兩名隊友在裂隙裡站滿六秒，就能封住這一道，全隊回復 8 點生命，反擊傷害提高五秒。三道封完，守門者才會出現。

一局大約兩分鐘。招募隊員湊出羈絆（例如星盾護衛加迴響歌者，圓陣時每秒回血），出擊前在整備頁選先發與起始節點。

離線可玩、沒有廣告、沒有內購。

- **關鍵字（100 字元內）**：小隊,陣形,封隙,隊友站位,羈絆,動作,短局,離線,無內購

## English

- **Name**: Crackveil Vanguard: Three-Formation Seal
- **Subtitle**: Switch formation, hold the rift with your squad
- **Promotional text**: Shield walls, flanks, artillery marks. Read the attack, switch formation, and bring two squadmates into the rift to seal it.

**Description**

You lead a squad, not a lone hero. Every member has their own weapon, health and position. Press Q (or tap the formation button) to switch between Wedge, Ring and Scatter, and your squad physically moves into the new shape.

The rift opens in three stages, each paired with an attack:
- Shield push from the front: break it with Wedge
- Pincer from both flanks: Ring keeps your squad close to the captain and cuts each hit from 14 to 4
- Artillery marks: spread out with Scatter

Every danger zone shows the formation that answers it. Step onto the rift in the wrong formation and it backfires, resetting progress. In the right one, wait out the attack and hold the rift with two squadmates for six seconds to seal that stage: the squad heals 8 HP and counter damage rises for five seconds. Seal all three and the gatekeeper appears.

Runs last about two minutes. Recruit squadmates to form bonds, and pick your starting lineup and node before each sortie. Plays offline. No ads, no in-app purchases.

- **Keywords**: squad,formation,tactics,rift,seal,bonds,action,short run,offline

## 審查備註草稿（英文，送審時才用）

Crackveil Vanguard is a squad-formation action game built in Godot by a solo developer. Its core loop is not auto-attack survival: each of the three rift seals is paired with a specific enemy attack (frontal shield push, flank pincer, artillery marks). The player must read the attack, switch the whole squad to the formation that counters it (Wedge / Ring / Scatter), and physically bring the captain plus two living squadmates into the rift for six seconds. Wrong formations backfire and reset the seal. Danger zones display the required formation, and every wave reports its outcome on screen. A 60-second demo video recorded on device is attached. This build contains only Crackveil's own code and assets; engine and fonts are standard third-party components (Godot, Noto Sans CJK).
